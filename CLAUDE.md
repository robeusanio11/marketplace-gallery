## Writing good code

When solving a problem, its very important that you attempt to understand the root cause and describe it to me before attempting to solve it. It's not appropriate to generate a bunch of code in order to hide a problem without understanding the root cause, because this makes the product less maintainable over time.

When you're unsure about the intended behavior of something, you should ask me before proceeding. You should also feel free to explore the codebase at any time to understand it.
